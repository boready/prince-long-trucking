Before Scheduling Millings

Availability can change because asphalt millings are often tied to paving and removal projects. Confirm the source, amount and intended use before scheduling transportation.

Site preparation, grading and compaction affect performance. Hauling service does not replace advice from a qualified contractor regarding drainage, base conditions or installation.

Common Planning Questions

  • Is the material currently available?
  • How many loads are expected?
  • Can trucks safely enter and turn around?
  • Who will direct placement on site?
  • Is compaction equipment ready?
