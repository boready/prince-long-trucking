Plan the Delivery Before Ordering Material

Material names and sizes can vary by supplier. Confirm the exact product with the quarry or material source, then provide the pickup location, quantity and delivery instructions.

Driveway strength, drainage and final appearance depend on the selected material, base preparation and compaction. Prince & Long Trucking provides hauling and delivery; customers should rely on their contractor or supplier for engineering and installation recommendations.
