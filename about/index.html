Professional from dispatch to delivery

Every job is approached with clear communication, responsible equipment operation and attention to the conditions that keep a site moving efficiently. Hauling is coordinated around the material, quantity, route, access requirements and requested schedule.

How we approach every haul

  • Safety first: Responsible equipment operation, load handling and jobsite awareness.
  • Clear communication: Direct coordination on timing, access, material and changing site conditions.
  • Jobsite ready: Registration, insurance and vendor documentation are available to qualified customers during onboarding.
  • Reliable coordination: One-time deliveries and recurring contractor loads are scheduled according to availability.

Who we serve

Prince & Long Trucking supports contractors, farms, municipalities, public works teams and property owners with rock, gravel, dirt, sand, asphalt millings and related hauling needs.

How They Work

A Reputation Built on the Work

Prince & Long Trucking is a Wamego-based hauling company serving Northeast Kansas. Customers know the team for being hard-working, professional and prompt—qualities that matter when materials, crews and equipment all need to stay on schedule.

The company supports construction, rural, agricultural, commercial and property projects with dump truck hauling, aggregate delivery and related transportation services.

Work Ethic

Every request is treated like an important part of the customer’s project, from a single delivery to ongoing hauling support.

Professional Conduct

Clear communication, responsible operation and respect for customers, suppliers and jobsites.

Prompt Service

Practical scheduling and timely coordination intended to reduce delays and keep work moving.

What Customers Can Expect

Before a load is scheduled, the team considers material type, quantity, route, truck access, site conditions and timing. Providing these details early helps reduce surprises and supports a smoother delivery.

Prince & Long Trucking does not rely on exaggerated claims. The company’s message is simple: work hard, communicate professionally and provide prompt service whenever scheduling and project conditions allow.
