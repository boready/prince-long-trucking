Choosing the Right Material for Your Northeast Kansas Project

The right material depends on drainage, traffic, soil conditions, project use and budget. This guide gives Northeast Kansas property owners and contractors a practical starting point before requesting delivery.

Request PricingCall (785) 844-5001

Rock and gravel for driveways and access roads

Crushed limestone, road rock and gravel are common choices for driveway gravel delivery in Wamego, Manhattan and Junction City. A compactable base can improve stability, while cleaner rock may be better where drainage matters.

Fill dirt and topsoil serve different purposes

Fill dirt is generally used to build grade, fill low areas and prepare building pads. Topsoil is better suited to lawns, gardens and finish grading where plant growth matters.

Sand and aggregate for drainage and construction

Sand can support concrete, bedding, drainage and landscaping applications. The correct gradation should be confirmed with the project contractor or material supplier.

Asphalt millings for rural lanes and driveways

Recycled asphalt millings can provide an economical surface for farm lanes, parking areas and long driveways when properly placed and compacted.
