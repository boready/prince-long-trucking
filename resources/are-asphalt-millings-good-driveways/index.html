Are Asphalt Millings Good for Driveways?

Learn when recycled asphalt millings may work for driveways and what preparation and compaction are needed.

They can create a firm recycled surface

Quality millings placed over a stable base can make an economical driveway or parking surface. They are often attractive for long rural lanes where new asphalt is not practical.

Material consistency varies

Millings can range from fine processed material to chunks with mixed debris. Ask about the source, particle size and whether the product has been screened or crushed.

Compaction improves performance

Spreading at a consistent depth and compacting with appropriate equipment helps the particles lock together. Simply dumping and driving over the pile rarely produces the same result.

Drainage remains essential

A crowned surface, maintained ditches and properly sized culverts help keep water from undermining the lane. Millings are not a waterproof substitute for drainage design.

Questions to answer before ordering

Be ready to provide the project address, measurements, desired material, estimated quantity, preferred delivery window and photos of the access route. Mention gates, soft ground, overhead utilities, steep slopes, culverts and limited turnaround areas. These details help prevent delays and allow the hauler to determine whether the requested placement is safe.

Local planning for Northeast Kansas

Soil conditions and weather can change quickly across Wamego, Manhattan, Junction City and nearby communities. Heavy rain may soften access routes, while freeze-thaw cycles can expose drainage and base problems. Build flexibility into the schedule and avoid placing valuable material before the site is ready to receive, spread and compact it.
