Spring Gravel Driveway Maintenance Checklist

Use this spring checklist to inspect drainage, potholes, culverts and gravel depth after Northeast Kansas winter weather.

Use this spring checklist to inspect drainage, potholes, culverts and gravel depth after Northeast Kansas winter weather.

Planning note: Material selection and quantities vary by site. Confirm product specifications with your supplier or contractor before ordering.
In this guide
  1. Inspect after the thaw
  2. Clear drainage paths
  3. Restore crown and shoulders
  4. Schedule material before peak season

Inspect after the thaw

Look for soft spots, pumping water, ruts and areas where frost movement disturbed the surface. Avoid heavy grading while the base is saturated.

Clear drainage paths

Remove leaves, sediment and debris from ditches and culverts. Water should leave the driveway quickly without crossing the travel surface.

Restore crown and shoulders

Grade displaced rock back toward the center and correct edges that trap water. Add material only after the shape and drainage problems are addressed.

Schedule material before peak season

Spring demand can increase quickly. Measure damaged areas, select the likely product and request delivery before contractors' schedules fill.

Questions to answer before ordering

Be ready to provide the project address, measurements, desired material, estimated quantity, preferred delivery window and photos of the access route. Mention gates, soft ground, overhead utilities, steep slopes, culverts and limited turnaround areas. These details help prevent delays and allow the hauler to determine whether the requested placement is safe.

Local planning for Northeast Kansas

Soil conditions and weather can change quickly across Wamego, Manhattan, Junction City and nearby communities. Heavy rain may soften access routes, while freeze-thaw cycles can expose drainage and base problems. Build flexibility into the schedule and avoid placing valuable material before the site is ready to receive, spread and compact it.
