How Long Do Asphalt Millings Last?

Understand what determines the service life of an asphalt millings driveway, including base, drainage, traffic and maintenance.

In this guide
  1. There is no single lifespan
  2. Heavy traffic increases wear
  3. Water shortens service life

There is no single lifespan

A well-prepared millings surface can serve for years, while material placed over weak soil may rut quickly. Performance varies with climate, traffic and installation quality.

Heavy traffic increases wear

Loaded trucks, trailers and sharp turns place greater stress on the surface than passenger vehicles. Adequate depth and a strong base are necessary in high-load areas.

Water shortens service life

Standing water softens the underlying soil and carries away fine particles. Maintaining crown, shoulders and drainage structures protects the investment.

Periodic maintenance is normal

Low areas may need additional material, grading or recompaction. Early repair prevents small drainage paths and ruts from becoming major failures.

Questions to answer before ordering

Be ready to provide the project address, measurements, desired material, estimated quantity, preferred delivery window and photos of the access route. Mention gates, soft ground, overhead utilities, steep slopes, culverts and limited turnaround areas. These details help prevent delays and allow the hauler to determine whether the requested placement is safe.

Local planning for Northeast Kansas

Soil conditions and weather can change quickly across Wamego, Manhattan, Junction City and nearby communities. Heavy rain may soften access routes, while freeze-thaw cycles can expose drainage and base problems. Build flexibility into the schedule and avoid placing valuable material before the site is ready to receive, spread and compact it.
