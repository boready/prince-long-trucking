What Types of Equipment Can Be Hauled Locally?

Review common farm and construction equipment that can be transported locally, from skid steers to compact excavators and attachments.

Compact construction equipment

Skid steers, compact track loaders, mini excavators, trenchers and small rollers are frequently moved between contractors, rental yards and job sites.

Farm and property equipment

Tractors, implements, mowers and selected agricultural equipment may be transported when dimensions, weight and loading requirements match the available trailer.

Attachments and specialty loads

Buckets, forks, augers, pallets and machinery can often travel with the primary machine if weight distribution and securement remain compliant.

Every move needs verification

Trailer capacity, axle weights, permits, route restrictions and securement points determine whether a load can be accepted. Send model numbers and photos for an accurate evaluation.

Questions to answer before ordering

Be ready to provide the project address, measurements, desired material, estimated quantity, preferred delivery window and photos of the access route. Mention gates, soft ground, overhead utilities, steep slopes, culverts and limited turnaround areas. These details help prevent delays and allow the hauler to determine whether the requested placement is safe.

Local planning for Northeast Kansas

Soil conditions and weather can change quickly across Wamego, Manhattan, Junction City and nearby communities. Heavy rain may soften access routes, while freeze-thaw cycles can expose drainage and base problems. Build flexibility into the schedule and avoid placing valuable material before the site is ready to receive, spread and compact it.
