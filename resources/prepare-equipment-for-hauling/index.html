How to Prepare Equipment for Transport

Prepare skid steers, compact excavators, tractors and attachments for safe local equipment hauling in Northeast Kansas.

Provide accurate dimensions and weight

The hauler needs the machine's operating weight, width, height and length, including attachments. Incorrect information can lead to the wrong trailer or permit requirements.

Secure loose items

Remove or secure buckets, tools, chains, doors and personal items. Fold mirrors or structures only according to the manufacturer's instructions.

Check fluids and condition

Repair active leaks and confirm that the machine can start, steer and brake for loading. Tell the hauler in advance if equipment is disabled or requires winching.

Prepare the loading area

Use firm, level ground with enough room for the truck and trailer. Keep workers clear during loading and follow the driver's direction around ramps and securement points.

Questions to answer before ordering

Be ready to provide the project address, measurements, desired material, estimated quantity, preferred delivery window and photos of the access route. Mention gates, soft ground, overhead utilities, steep slopes, culverts and limited turnaround areas. These details help prevent delays and allow the hauler to determine whether the requested placement is safe.

Local planning for Northeast Kansas

Soil conditions and weather can change quickly across Wamego, Manhattan, Junction City and nearby communities. Heavy rain may soften access routes, while freeze-thaw cycles can expose drainage and base problems. Build flexibility into the schedule and avoid placing valuable material before the site is ready to receive, spread and compact it.
